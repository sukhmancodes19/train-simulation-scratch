Train Simulation (Scratch)

A train animation/simulation built in Scratch.

Play it online: https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/860076536/

How to run:

1. Online: open the link above and click the green flag.
2. From this repo: download Train.sb3, open https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor/, then choose File > Load from your computer.

What's inside:

1. A train sprite that glides across the stage with music when the scene starts
2. Characters and text slides (t1–t4) across 5 backdrops
3. Press space to hide the train
